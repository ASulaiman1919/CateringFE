Degi Kitchen is a home-based catering kitchen in Ashburn, Virginia. Our cooking begins with Afghan favorites: fragrant rice, kababs, slow-cooked meats, dumplings, and fresh sides.

We prepare food for family tables, office lunches, and celebrations. Iranian, Pakistani, and Middle Eastern favorites can also be part of a custom menu.
